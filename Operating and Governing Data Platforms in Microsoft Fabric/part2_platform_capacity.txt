Other workloads are not the concern. Power BI peaks at 81% during 08:00-10:00 and does not overlap with the overnight peaks. Data Warehouse (43% average) and Data Science (19%) are low and stable.

Impact: no throttling, rejected operations, failed refreshes, or delayed queries occurred, so users were unaffected. However, a 92% peak leaves little headroom, and further workload growth or longer runtimes could cause throttling. Successful completion does not prove efficient use.

Actions: stagger the 02:00 pipeline start times to spread load, optimize Sales Transformation, and add utilization alerts around 85-90%. Investigate the fourth peak, whose cause the evidence does not show. Increasing capacity is not justified yet.
