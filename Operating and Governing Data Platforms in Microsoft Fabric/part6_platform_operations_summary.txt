Overall operational health
The environment is stable but showing early warning signs rather than active failure. No outages, throttling, failed report refreshes, or unauthorized activity were recorded, and Customer Ingestion and Executive Reporting Refresh run reliably. However, runtime growth, limited capacity headroom, stale access, and overdue data cleanup suggest operational discipline is lagging behind platform growth.
